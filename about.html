ABOUT BEYOND SMP

WHAT IS
BEYOND?

ONE WORLD. MANY STORIES.

Beyond SMP is a Minecraft survival multiplayer server inspired by Attack on Titan, Avatar: The Last Airbender and Minecraft itself. Players can choose their path, form alliances, build communities and create their own stories inside an evolving world.

TRUST IS EARNED.

Factions have their own goals, powers and identities. Alliances can change, rivalries can grow and the choices players make can shape what happens next.

THE THREE PATHS

Paradis, Benders and Titan Shifters are the major faction paths currently planned for Beyond SMP.
